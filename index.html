Mail Manager

A personal email and calendar assistant used only by its author, Zhiyi Wang.

What it does

  • Sorts the author's university mail and sends short reminders for important messages.
  • Reads booking confirmations (train, flight, hotel) in the author's Gmail, read-only.
  • Adds the corresponding events to the author's own Google Calendar.

Google access

The app requests gmail.readonly and calendar.events for one account only. It never sends, deletes or modifies email, and it is not offered to other users.
