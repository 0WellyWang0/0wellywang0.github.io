Privacy policy

Mail Manager · last updated 10 October 2026

Who uses it

Mail Manager is a private tool run by Zhiyi Wang on his own computer for his own Google account. It has no other users.

Data accessed

  • Gmail (read-only): messages that look like bookings or tickets are read to find dates and times.
  • Google Calendar: events are created or updated in the owner's calendar.

How data is handled

  • Data is processed on the owner's computer; message text may be sent to an AI model (Anthropic Claude) solely to extract events.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any other purpose.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
